Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What is SpendWise?

SpendWise is a personal finance dashboard that helps you view and manage your spending in one place.

How do I view my transactions?

Open the Transactions section from the sidebar or use the Transactions link on your dashboard.

How can I change my preferences?

Visit Settings to update your currency, appearance and notification preferences. Select Save Changes to store your selections.

How do I enable dark mode?

Open Settings and turn on the Dark Mode switch. Save your changes to keep the preference.

Where can I update my profile?

Select Profile from the sidebar to view and update your profile information.
